Does one sensor explain the majority of variance?

For each objective, the root split is the tree's single most informative split. "Root split alone" is the variance it explains by itself (training R² at depth 1); "full tree" is the variance explained once the tree reaches its optimal depth. Values are means across the 12 cost-threshold configurations per objective. The sensor label shows which physical location was most often chosen as the root, and in how many of the 12 configurations.

Reading it. A tall blue bar close to the green bar means one sensor is doing almost all the work (corn & beans water-content objectives: ~65-70% of the full tree's explanatory power comes from a single, consistently-chosen sensor). A short blue bar far below the green means no single sensor dominates — the tree needs many splits, and/or the identity of the top sensor changes as the cost penalty shifts (both crops' flux objectives; corn's combined flux+water-content objective).
