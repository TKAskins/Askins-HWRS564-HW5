Sensor selection consistency, by crop & objective

For each crop–objective pair, 12 trees were fit (the no-cost baseline plus 11 cost-weighting values 0.0–1.0). For each tree, the set of physical sensors used at that tree's own explained-variance-peak level was recorded. A sensor's selection frequency is the fraction of those 12 trees that used it. Tiers: selected by all 12 trees, by more than 75% (but not all), by more than 50% (up to 75%), and by 50% or fewer.
