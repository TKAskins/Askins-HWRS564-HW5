Importance is the weighted-average fraction of total variance explained by all splits on this sensor location, averaged across all 12 trained trees (baseline + cost thresholds 0.0–1.0) for each objective, weighted by each tree's training variance explained. Standalone cost is the sum of initial setup, sensor unit, depth installation, and per-measurement costs assuming no other sensors exist — the maximum marginal cost of adding this sensor to a network. Efficiency (diagonal guide lines) = importance % ÷ cost. Sensors to the upper-left are more cost-effective; those to the lower-right deliver less accuracy per unit cost.
